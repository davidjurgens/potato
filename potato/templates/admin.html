How fast is the work going?

Active time is measured from each annotator's own interaction events, with gaps longer than five minutes capped so an idle tab does not count as work. Only saved items count. When the review workflow is on, first-pass approval is the share of an annotator's reviewed items that were finished without being sent back, and rework is the share sent back at least once.

Did AI assistance change the answers?

A random share of each annotator's items (ai_support.holdout) showed no AI assistance. Each scheme compares those answers with the assisted ones: how often they match the model's own answer, how often they match gold where there is one, and how much annotators agree within each group. Higher model agreement with assistance, without higher gold accuracy, is the signature of anchoring.
